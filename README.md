# 👟 Foot Locker vs StockX: The Evolution of the Sneaker Industry

## 📊 About This Project
This Power BI project analyzes how StockX’s customer-centric, trend-forward approach has challenged Foot Locker’s traditional retail dominance. Through five interactive pages, I explore market trends, brand positioning, financial response strategies, and the rise of culture-first commerce.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Excel (data prep)
- SQL Server (data cleaning & aggregation)
- Power BI (data modeling, DAX, and visualization)

## 🧩 Dashboard Overview
**1. Intro Page**  
📌 Title slide with navigation — sets the stage for the analysis.

**2. Market Evolution**  
📈 Total sales, cash flow, and user growth show how StockX is rising against Foot Locker’s established presence.

**3. Customer vs Product Focus**  
📦 Foot Locker leans on mass inventory, while StockX rides consumer demand and exclusivity.

**4. Financial Pressure & Brand Response**  
💰 Lace Up Plan KPIs and competitive revenue breakdown reveal Foot Locker's reactive strategy.

**5. Insights & Recommendations**  
🧠 Final takeaways and strategic recommendations for the future of retail in a culture-driven economy.

## 💡 Key Insights
- StockX grew by prioritizing cultural demand and digital accessibility.
- Foot Locker retains market presence but must evolve toward digital-native strategies.
- The future of sneakers lies in demand-first, customer-centric retail models.
